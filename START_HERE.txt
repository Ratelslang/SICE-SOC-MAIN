❓ COMMON QUESTIONS:

Q: Do I need to set anything up?
A: No! Just download the files and double-click.

Q: Where should I put the files?
A: Anywhere! Desktop, Documents folder, Downloads, etc.
   Just keep SICE_SOC_MAIN.html and OPS_.html together.

Q: Will it work offline?
A: Yes! 100% offline except PDF export needs internet.

Q: Can I use this on my phone?
A: Yes! It's responsive. Email the .html file to yourself,
   open in phone browser, works perfectly.

Q: How do I make it easy to open?
A: Option 1: Right-click SICE_SOC_MAIN.html → "Create Shortcut"
             Drag shortcut to Desktop
   Option 2: Double-click LAUNCH_SOC_MAIN.bat

Q: What if Reports Hub doesn't show?
A: Try F5 to refresh. Make sure you're using modern browser
   (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge). Works on all of them.

Q: Are vCards safe?
A: Yes! Only contain: Name, Phone, Title, Company, Address.
   No sensitive data. Safe to share with entire team.
